Status:

```text
REFUND_WITHDRAWAL_POLICY: DRAFT_ONLY
PUBLIC_RELEASE: BLOCKED
```

Consumer-law review is required before any public-facing refund, cancellation or withdrawal wording is used.

Digital products, templates, setup services, support services and custom work may require different withdrawal, refund and performance-start decisions.

No final consumer wording is approved by this draft. Any public offer must be reviewed with the provider-information, privacy, payment, tax and invoice decisions.
