# Privacy Notice Template

Status:

```text
PRIVACY_NOTICE: TEMPLATE_ONLY
PUBLIC_RELEASE: BLOCKED_UNTIL_COMPLETED_AND_REVIEWED
LEGAL_REVIEW: REQUIRED
REAL_CASE_DATA: PROHIBITED
```

This template is for preparing a public privacy notice before repository visibility, support channels, contact forms, payment pages or commercial offers are made public.

It is not legal advice. Replace all bracketed placeholders and review before public use.

## Processing Scope

This public layer is designed for synthetic examples only. It must not receive or process real case files, government letters, credentials, medical documents, private family data or protected-core material.

Potential data categories before public release must be reviewed:

- contact data from inquiries
- communication content from emails or forms
- payment metadata from payment providers
- technical log data from hosting or platform providers
- security-report data if someone reports a vulnerability
- business customer data if a separate setup agreement is used

## Data Subject Rights

The final notice must explain rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent where applicable, plus the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## No Automated Decision-Making

Default public-safe statement:

```text
No automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effect are made in the public layer.
```

## Real-Case Submission Warning

Recommended public wording:

```text
Do not send real case files, legal letters, health data, credentials, government documents or private third-party data through public channels. The public layer is synthetic-data-only and does not provide legal advice or emergency support.
```

## Review Checklist

Before public release, confirm:

- controller identity is complete
- purposes are complete
- legal bases are reviewed
- payment provider posture is reviewed
- recipient / processor list is complete
- third-country transfer posture is reviewed
- storage periods are defined
- user rights are explained
- security contact is separated from general contact
- real-case-data warning is visible
- no protected-core data appears in public docs
