# Paid Services Terms Draft

Status:

```text
PAID_SERVICES_TERMS: DRAFT_ONLY
PUBLIC_RELEASE: BLOCKED
HUMAN_APPROVAL: NOT_GIVEN
```

Paid work requires a separate written agreement that defines scope, deliverables, price, payment terms, taxes, invoices, consumer rights and review responsibilities.

Paid work does not create legal-advice duties, does not provide emergency support, does not guarantee response times unless expressly agreed in writing and does not approve public release.

Paid work does not grant access to protected-core material unless a separate written agreement expressly allows it.

This draft does not provide final payment, tax, invoice or consumer-rights wording. Those topics require separate owner and legal review before any public release or public offer.
