# Value and Sustainability Policy

## Principle

Public work should create real benefit and remain sustainable for the builder.

## Public Layer

This repository may provide public-safe documentation, synthetic examples, tests and non-production review-gate logic.

## Protected Layer

Production workflows, premium templates, implementation guides, custom automations, client-specific logic and commercial setup methodology may remain private, paid or separately licensed.

## Commercial Use

Commercial use may require written permission, a commercial license, paid setup, support agreement or implementation contract.

## Fair Value Exchange

```text
Public trust.
Protected value.
Fair exchange.
Sustainable work.
```
