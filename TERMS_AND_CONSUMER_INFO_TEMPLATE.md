## Offer Types

### Voluntary Support / Donation

Purpose:

- support public maintenance
- sponsor development
- no direct individual entitlement unless separately agreed

Required public wording:

```text
Voluntary support payments help maintain the project. They do not create legal-advice duties, emergency support, public-release approval, production readiness, protected-core access or a guaranteed response time.
```

### Local Setup Service

Suggested scope:

```text
Local installation, synthetic-demo walkthrough, configuration orientation and safe-use boundary setup.
```

Excluded:

- legal advice
- real-case review
- final deadline calculation
- court or authority communication
- filing / submission
- emergency support

### B2B Workflow Implementation

Suggested scope:

```text
Review-gate workflow setup, evidence-card taxonomy, internal documentation structure, local-first process design and safety-boundary documentation.
```

Excluded unless separately agreed and legally reviewed:

- individual legal case assessment
- representation
- regulated professional services
- productive external-action automation

### Digital Templates / Documents

Suggested scope:

```text
General-purpose templates and documentation packs for self-guided workflow structuring.
```

Mandatory caution:

```text
Templates are general information and workflow aids. They are not tailored legal advice and do not replace qualified review for individual cases.
```

## Minimum Commercial Contract Fields

Before taking paid work, the offer or agreement should define:

- provider identity
- customer identity
- exact scope
- exclusions
- price and taxes
- payment due date
- delivery criteria
- communication channel
- support limits
- refund / cancellation / withdrawal posture
- confidentiality
- data handling
- no-legal-advice boundary unless a qualified legal provider is involved
- no emergency support

## Safe Checkout Wording

Recommended payment-page wording:

```text
By paying, you support or purchase the described non-legal setup / workflow service only. This payment does not create legal representation, emergency legal support, protected-core access, public-release approval, court or authority filing, or a guarantee of legal outcome.
```

## Stop Rules

Do not use public paid offers if:

- provider data is incomplete
- tax / invoice handling is unclear
- B2C withdrawal wording is missing
- offer scope suggests legal advice
- payment page suggests emergency support
- support expectations are undefined
- privacy notice is incomplete
- real-case data collection is possible without reviewed legal basis
