This repository is synthetic-only. It must not receive personal data, real case files, private letters, account data, screenshots with identifying details or other sensitive material.

Public issues must not contain personal data, real case facts, names, addresses, file numbers, credentials, secrets or private documents.

Security and privacy reports should avoid sensitive details in public channels. A private reporting process must be decided before any public release.

This is not a final privacy notice. Final privacy wording requires a provider decision, review of actual contact channels and review of any payment, support or reporting workflows before publication.
