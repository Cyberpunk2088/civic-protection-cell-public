## Deal Flow

Recommended flow:

1. Public repository builds trust.
2. Support link funds maintenance.
3. Inquiry form collects non-sensitive commercial inquiries only.
4. Separate written scope is sent for paid setup.
5. Payment only after scope, privacy, invoice and tax posture are clear.
6. Delivery uses synthetic examples unless a separate reviewed data agreement exists.

## Qualification Questions

Ask prospects:

- Are you looking for technical setup or legal advice?
- Will you use synthetic examples or real case data?
- Are you B2B or B2C?
- Do you need local installation, workflow design or documentation structure?
- Do you need a lawyer or qualified legal professional involved?
- What must be delivered for the project to be accepted?

## Hard Stop Answers

If a prospect asks for any of the following, stop and require legal review / qualified provider involvement:

- legal opinion on a concrete case
- final deadline calculation
- court filing
- authority communication
- representation
- emergency legal action
- real personal data ingestion without reviewed data process
- automatic sending or submission
