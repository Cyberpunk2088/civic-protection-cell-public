# Support

## Public Contact

General contact, security contact and commercial inquiries currently use the owner-supplied public contact route documented in `PROVIDER_INFO_DRAFT.md`.

## Support Boundaries

Support for this public repository is limited to:

- documentation questions
- local setup questions
- synthetic demo usage
- safety-boundary clarification
- public-safe issue reporting

Support does not include:

- legal advice
- legal representation
- emergency support
- final deadline calculation
- court, authority or third-party communication
- real case processing
- production deployment
- protected-core access

## Paid Work Boundary

Paid work, setup help or workflow support requires a separate written agreement defining:

- scope
- price
- deliverables
- timeline
- data boundary
- tax and invoice handling
- consumer or withdrawal terms where applicable

Payment alone does not create legal advice duties, representation, emergency support, deadline guarantees, production readiness or protected-core access.

## Sensitive Data

Do not send through public issues, comments, PayPal notes or public channels:

- real case files
- legal documents
- health data
- third-party personal data
- credentials
- secrets
- tax numbers
- bank data
- protected-core material

## Security Reports

Do not disclose sensitive vulnerability details publicly.

Until a dedicated private security channel is created, use the public contact route in `PROVIDER_INFO_DRAFT.md` with a minimal, non-sensitive summary first.
